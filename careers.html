Why alumni will actually use it

The strongest version of this page feels polished, simple, and useful right away. It should make it easy for alumni to contribute without needing to dig through emails or scattered forms.

A

Low-friction contribution

One button to post a role. One form to volunteer as a mentor. One place to revisit whenever they want to help.

B

Professional presentation

The page reflects the same premium black-and-gold Kappa Rho aesthetic as the rest of the alumni site while feeling career-focused and credible.

C

Long-term chapter value

It turns alumni engagement into something tangible: jobs, mentorship, referrals, and stronger brotherhood after graduation.
